No. 01 of 52

Concussion

Stars tingling over the steep cliffs
of my arms. At the borderline of table
this morning, an event clicked,
beyond reach.

Miscarriage of an apple makes life quit
even harder.
We ponder on how pure magic can be poured
from a withered spoon
through which, fish oils glittered in sorrow.

As if it was originated to be uncontrolled.
As if we call fallen apples cowardice.

— 凡亞
