No. 03 of 52

drive

to nowhereville without
a driver or is this life even possible
without it tee tee daa
fire of my loin of cosmos
he murmurs
in the backseat
sweating trembling — then
the book drops and destiny
is handed to an explosion
passing Francesca through:

sometimes she forgets
she could also drive
in the autopilot mode

— 凡亞
