About

凡亞 writes poems that move between Chinese, English, and fragments of other languages — across cities, memory, myth, and the ordinary. This site collects that work in one place: fifty-five poems, presented as they were written, without translation or explanation.

The poems are arranged in the order of the original manuscript. Use the index to browse by title, or search to find a poem by name.
